R · TIME SERIES FORECASTING

Forecasting U.S. Gasoline Station Sales

Compared forecasting methods across 359 monthly observations and selected Holt-Winters for near-term planning, with a 2.792% MAPE.

R Holt-Winters ARIMA
Read case study

My team analyzed U.S. gasoline station sales from 1996 through 2025. We compared regression models with trend and seasonality, a two-level model, Holt-Winters exponential smoothing, and ARIMA to forecast the next 13 months.

Holt-Winters was selected after evaluating the models using RMSE and MAPE. Its 2.792% MAPE and responsiveness to recent shifts made it the most useful choice for a changing market.

WEB SCRAPING · DATA ANALYTICS

AI vs. Data Science Job Market Analysis

Analyzed 920 job postings to compare in-demand skills, compensation, and location—revealing a $39K Bay Area median-salary premium.

Python Selenium BeautifulSoup
Read case study

My team collected AI/ML and Data Science job-posting information using Selenium and BeautifulSoup, then transformed unstructured text into usable fields for skills, education, salary, work arrangement, and location.

We found substantial overlap in the two fields’ skill sets alongside meaningful compensation and geographic differences. The Bay Area had a $140K median salary, compared with $101K elsewhere in the U.S.
